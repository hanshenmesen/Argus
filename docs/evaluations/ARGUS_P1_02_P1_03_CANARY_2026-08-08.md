## P1-02 role-session policy

visible tests, independent Reviewer Skills, and held-out checks.

| Policy | Runs | Joint success | Mean wall | Mean prompt chars | Mean provider input | Mean repeated repo reads |

Mission reused less context and was faster, but failed held-out correctness twice while
Reviewer returned `done`. This rejects promotion: production remains `fresh`; `mission`
and `rolling` stay opt-in diagnostics. The lifecycle implementation itself is complete,
including explicit `repeated_contradiction`, `reviewer_confusion`, and
`quality_degradation` signals that rotate only the affected role capsule. No keyword
inference is used.

## P1-03 natural Skill invocation

Ten normal Engineer missions contained no instruction to inspect Skills. Each exposed one
high-fit Skill and one unrelated SQL Skill. The task set covered strict integer parsing
and ordered case-insensitive header merging; held-out checks exercised semantics omitted
from visible tests.

| Runs | Models | Useful body opened | Wrong body opened | Held-out pass | Mean wall | Known cost |
| ---: | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 10 | gpt-5.6-sol, gpt-5-mini | 10/10 | 0/10 | 10/10 | 39.97 s | $0.35752 |

A fair four-run matcher+injection baseline charged a real provider selection call before
the execution call. It passed 4/4, averaged 41.52 s, and cost $0.15856 total
($0.03964/run), versus $0.03575/run for natural on-demand across the ten-run canary.
This is directional rather than a population estimate, but unlike the earlier oracle it
includes selection cost. The earlier body-injection oracle remains the lower-bound
reference, not a deployable policy.

Legacy Reviewer `skill_ops` is removed from current state and knobs. A migration replay
proves old JSON verdicts containing the field remain readable; the obsolete operations
are ignored rather than reintroduced into runtime state.

Raw provider/session traces remain local because they contain host paths and provider
session material. Disclosure-safe aggregates and test fixtures are committed.
